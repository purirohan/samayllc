Website privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy covers the samay.llc website. Each of our apps has its own privacy policy, linked below.

What this website collects

Nothing. This site has no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, and no forms. We don't know who visits it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical information such as IP addresses to keep its service secure and running. That's covered by GitHub's privacy statement. We don't receive or use that information.

If you email us

We use your email address and message only to reply to you. We don't add you to a mailing list, and we don't sell or share your information.
