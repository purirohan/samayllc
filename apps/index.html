SnapRecaller

iPhone · iOS 26 · Free to try, one-time unlock · Coming soon to the App Store

Screenshots are how we save things for later, and then later never comes. SnapRecaller turns a screenshot into a reminder: share it, type when and why in one line (“fri 10am buy tickets”), and it comes back as a notification with the screenshot attached.

It can suggest a note by reading the screenshot with on-device AI, and nothing is ever uploaded.
