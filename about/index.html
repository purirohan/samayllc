About

An independent company built around respecting your time.

Samay LLC makes its own apps for iPhone and offers business strategy consulting. We're small on purpose: we'd rather do a few things carefully than a lot of things quickly.

The name

Samay (समय) means “time” in Hindi. It's the one thing everyone feels short on, and it shapes how we work. Our apps should save more time than they take, and our advice should get to the point.

What we believe

  • Respect people's time. Tools should get out of the way, and recommendations should be clear enough to act on.
  • Privacy by default. Our apps don't have accounts, ads, or analytics, and they keep your data on your device.
  • Honest pricing. Free to try, then a one-time purchase instead of a subscription.

What we make

Our first app is SnapRecaller, which turns screenshots into reminders. See all of our work on the Apps page, or read about our consulting.
